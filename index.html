02 · The essentials

What is IELTS?

IELTS stands for the International English Language Testing System. It measures how well people use English in academic and everyday contexts.

01 / LISTENING

Understand spoken English

Listen for the main idea, details, names, numbers, and changes in meaning.

02 / READING

Understand written texts

Find key information, identify the writer’s purpose, and manage time.

03 / WRITING

Express ideas clearly

Organise ideas, support arguments, and use accurate grammar and vocabulary.

04 / SPEAKING

Communicate naturally

Answer directly, develop ideas, and speak clearly rather than memorising scripts.

Remember: IELTS preparation is not only about learning test tricks. It is also about improving your real English ability.

03 · Start smart

Build a study routine that works

A good plan should be specific, flexible, and realistic. Start with your current level instead of copying someone else’s schedule.

STEP 01

Set a clear goal

Choose a target band score and a reasonable test timeline. Break the big goal into small weekly targets.

STEP 02

Check your current level

Try a short practice test and identify your strongest and weakest skills.

STEP 03

Practise in focused blocks

Study for 25–40 minutes, take a short break, and decide exactly what you will practise next.

STEP 04

Review and adapt

Keep an error log. Revisit mistakes and change your plan when something is not working.

04 · The four skills

Train each skill with a purpose

LISTENING

Listen actively

Use short clips. First listen for the main idea; listen again for details. Check the transcript only after trying.

PodcastsTranscripts

READING

Read strategically

Practise skimming for the main idea and scanning for specific facts. Support answers with evidence.

ArticlesTimed sets

WRITING

Plan before writing

Analyse the question, outline your ideas, write clearly, and leave time to check grammar and spelling.

OutlinesFeedback

SPEAKING

Speak and reflect

Record short answers, listen back, and notice pauses, unclear sounds, repeated words, and grammar patterns.

Voice notesMock answers

Choose practice materials that match your level. Increase difficulty gradually, and review every answer you get wrong.

06 · A sample week

A balanced weekly plan

Use this as a starting point, then adjust the time and difficulty to fit your schoolwork and energy.

DayMain focusSuggested task
MondayListeningOne short audio, answer review, and five new words.
TuesdayReadingOne timed passage; explain why each answer is correct.
WednesdaySpeakingRecord two answers and note one thing to improve.
ThursdayWritingPlan and write one response; check structure and grammar.
FridayVocabularyReview saved words and create original example sentences.
SaturdayMixed practiceComplete a short timed set and review mistakes carefully.
SundayRest and reflectReview progress, plan the next week, and take a proper break.

Quality matters more than filling every minute. A sustainable plan is easier to follow for months.

07 · Avoid these traps

Common mistakes to avoid

01

Memorising without understanding

Learn how a phrase works and create your own examples instead of copying it blindly.

02

Ignoring mistakes

Do not only check your score. Find the reason behind each wrong answer and practise that weakness.

03

Studying only one skill

Give all four skills regular attention, with extra time for the areas that need more work.

04

Using difficult materials too early

Build accuracy and confidence with suitable material before moving to harder tasks.

05

Expecting instant results

Track small improvements over time. Progress is often gradual, not perfectly linear.

08 · Final thoughts

Small steps. Real progress.

Effective IELTS preparation is a cycle: set a goal, practise deliberately, review the evidence, and try again.

TAKEAWAY 01

Be consistent

Build a routine you can repeat, even on busy days.

TAKEAWAY 02

Be active

Use English, test your understanding, and create your own examples.

TAKEAWAY 03

Be reflective

Use mistakes as information about what to practise next.

TAKEAWAY 04

Be patient

Give your skills time to develop and celebrate steady progress.

Thank you for listening. Keep learning, keep practising, and trust the process.
