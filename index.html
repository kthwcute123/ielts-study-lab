A practical framework

Study guide

Small, repeatable actions make vocabulary easier to remember.

STEP 01

Read for the main idea

Before looking up words, identify the topic and the purpose of each paragraph.

STEP 02

Choose useful vocabulary

Save words that appear repeatedly, help you express ideas, or are important to understanding the passage.

STEP 03

Learn sound and meaning together

Check IPA, listen to the pronunciation, and read the example sentence aloud.

STEP 04

Review actively

Hide the meaning and try to recall it. Return to your dictionary regularly rather than saving words and never reviewing them.
